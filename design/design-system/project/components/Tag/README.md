# Tag

A small solid label above a title, saying who or what it's for ("Up to age 5", "Self or plan-managed").

- Markup: `<span class="fmt-tag">`, plus `is-teal`, `is-sun`, `is-ochre` or `is-pink`. Purple is the default.
- Purple, teal, ochre (`sun-deep`) and pink (`pink-deep`) take white text, all 5:1 or better. Sun takes `ink`; never white on `sun`.
- One tag per title, above it and aligned with it.
