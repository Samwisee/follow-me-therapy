# Picker

Our clock and our calendar, on a card, for picking a time, a date or days of the week. They replace the device's own pickers, so they look like the rest of the app and work the same on every phone.

- **Clock (`TimePicker`):** the time large at the top. Tap the hour or the minutes to change that part, and pick am or pm from one switch (am over pm, two equal pill segments). The round dial on `picker-dial` shows the 12 hours, then the minutes in fives. Each one is a 44px target, and the chosen one is a `picker-selected` circle joined to the centre by the hand. Picking an hour moves on to the minutes, and the hand turns the short way round to each new choice (not with reduced motion).
- **Calendar (`DatePicker`):** the month and year, with ‹ › to move. Monday comes first, and each day is a 44px target. Today has a `picker-today` ring, and the chosen day is a purple circle with `picker-on-selected` words.
- **Days of the week (`DaysPicker`):** the calendar's week, Monday first, each day a 44px circle with a `picker-line` ring. Choose as many as you like (purple with paper words), or tap Weekdays or Every day. A `DaysField` opens it, showing the days as people say them (Weekdays, Mon, Wed, Fri).
- **The card:** `picker-fill` with a `picker-line` outline and `radius-card`, the title at the top, Cancel and Done at the foot.
- Every number and day is a button read out in full, like "7 o'clock" or "Tuesday 4 November, today".
- In code: `<TimePicker value="07:30" onDone={…} />`, `<DatePicker value="2026-11-04" onDone={…} />`. `<DaysPicker value={[0, 2, 4]} onDone={…} />`. A `TimeField` opens the clock when tapped, a `DaysField` the week.
