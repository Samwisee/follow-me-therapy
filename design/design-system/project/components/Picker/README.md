# Picker

Our clock and our calendar, on a card, for picking a time or a date. They replace the device's own pickers, so they look like the rest of the app and work the same on every phone.

- **Clock (`TimePicker`):** the time large at the top. Tap the hour or the minutes to change that part, and pick am or pm from two chips. The round dial on `picker-dial` shows the 12 hours, then the minutes in fives. Each one is a 44px target, and the chosen one is a `picker-selected` circle joined to the centre by the hand. Picking an hour moves on to the minutes.
- **Calendar (`DatePicker`):** the month and year, with ‹ › to move. Monday comes first, and each day is a 44px target. Today has a `picker-today` ring, and the chosen day is a purple circle with `picker-on-selected` words.
- **The card:** `picker-fill` with a `picker-line` outline and `radius-card`, the title at the top, Cancel and Done at the foot.
- Every number and day is a button read out in full, like "7 o'clock" or "Tuesday 4 November, today".
- In code: `<TimePicker value="07:30" onDone={…} />`, `<DatePicker value="2026-11-04" onDone={…} />`. A `TimeField` opens the clock when tapped.
