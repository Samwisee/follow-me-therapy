What is Occupational Therapy?

“Occupational Therapy (OT) is about the activities we do each day, what brings us joy, and the journey to becoming you.”

Paediatric OT helps children and young people build the skills for play, school, self-care and everyday independence.

OT can help your child:

  • Improve fine and gross motor skills
  • Understand their sensory systems
  • Develop play and engagement skills
  • Regulate their emotions
  • Build self-care and independence
  • Get ready for school
  • Enjoy mealtimes and manage fussy eating

Every session is a space for real-life problem solving, so you and your family feel in control of the therapy journey.

Meet Simone

Simone Gabriele

Paediatric Occupational Therapist
Master of Occupational Therapy, La Trobe University

Simone began her career in early intervention, then moved into neurodevelopmental community services, where she led teams and supervised new OTs.

Her practice is strength-based and neurodiversity-affirming, with your child at the centre. She believes therapy should be fun, engaging and practical for busy families.

Telehealth across Australia

See your therapist by video or phone, with no travel. Therapy fits into your family routine, wherever you live, including rural and regional areas.

It also reaches beyond the home:

  • Coaching calls with grandparents or carers
  • Skill-building programs for support workers
  • Working with your child’s teacher
  • Support while you wait for face-to-face services
Sessions run on Microsoft Teams.

Research backs it up: a 2023 review of 18 trials found telehealth OT for children works well alongside face-to-face therapy, and families were happy with it. Read the review
