Occupational Therapy

Occupational therapy (OT) is about the activities we do each day, what brings us joy, and the journey to becoming you.

We help children and young people build the skills for independence and everyday life, with therapy shaped around each child.

OT can help your child:

  • Improve fine and gross motor skills
  • Understand their sensory systems
  • Develop play and engagement skills
  • Regulate their emotions
  • Build self-care and independence
  • Get ready for school
  • Enjoy mealtimes and manage fussy eating

Every session is a space for real-life problem solving, so you and your family feel in control of the therapy journey.

Telehealth

Meet your therapist by video or phone, with no travel. Telehealth fits into your family routine and brings therapy to you anywhere in Australia, including rural and regional areas.

It also reaches beyond the home:

  • Coaching calls with grandparents or carers
  • Skill-building programs for support workers
  • Working with your child’s teacher
  • Support while you wait for face-to-face services
Sessions run on Microsoft Teams.

Meet the Team

Simone Gabriele

Occupational Therapist · Master of Occupational Therapy, La Trobe University

Simone began her career in early intervention before moving into neurodevelopmental community services, where she led teams and supervised the next generation of OTs.

Her practice is strength-based and neurodiversity-affirming, with each child at the centre. She believes therapy should be fun, engaging and practical for busy families.

Experience with:

  • Autism (ASD)
  • ADHD
  • Behaviour support needs
  • Global developmental delay
  • Cerebral palsy
  • Down syndrome
  • Genetic and neurodevelopmental disabilities
  • Motor and coordination difficulties
