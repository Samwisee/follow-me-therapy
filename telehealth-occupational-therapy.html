For rural, regional and remote families

If the nearest children’s OT is hours away, or the local waitlist is long, telehealth brings therapy to you. I work with families across every state and territory, from regional towns to remote properties and communities.

  • No travel: sessions happen at home, at the kitchen table or in the backyard
  • Times that suit your time zone, including Western Australia and the Northern Territory
  • Coaching for the people around your child: parents, grandparents, teachers, distance-education supervisors and support workers
  • Reports and therapy plans for NDIS, Medicare and school, written for your child’s goals
  • A slower connection is fine: sessions can switch to phone or use activities planned ahead

Who telehealth suits

Telehealth works for many families, including:

  • Families in rural and regional areas
  • Busy families who want to skip the travel
  • Children who feel more settled at home
  • Families waiting for face-to-face services

What a session looks like

  1. 1

    Join from home

    Connect on Microsoft Teams from a laptop, tablet or phone.

  2. 2

    Work on real goals

    Your therapist works with you and your child using everyday activities and things you already have at home.

  3. 3

    Keep it going

    You get practical strategies to use between sessions, so progress continues all week.

Telehealth goes beyond the home

  • Coaching calls with grandparents or carers
  • Skill-building programs for support workers
  • Working with your child’s teacher
  • Support while you wait for face-to-face services

Research backs it up: a 2025 review of 18 studies found early therapy by telehealth, OT included, helped young children about as well as in person, and families were happy with it. Read the review

What you’ll need

  • A device with a camera and microphone
  • A reliable internet connection
  • A quiet space with room to move
  • Microsoft Teams (free to use)
