Therapy that fits your plan

5 Steps Forward

A focused 5-session program for specific goals. Great for a boost, or trying OT for the first time.

Steady Steps

A 12-session block of structured, consistent support for bigger goals. Blocks can be stacked.

Early Start Denver Model

Play-based therapy for children up to 5 with autism or developmental delays. About ESDM

Assessments and reports

  • NDIS Access Report
  • Functional Capacity Assessment (FCA)
  • Progress Report
  • Sensory Processing Assessment
  • Assistive Technology (AT), with suitability reviewed at the initial assessment

How it works

  1. Make a referral

    Complete our referral form and include your plan details.

  2. Initial assessment

    We get to know your child and family, and set goals that link to your plan.

  3. Therapy

    Telehealth or community sessions, tailored to those goals.

  4. Reports

    Progress reports help at plan reviews.
